# 2026-10-08

The second release, v0.2.0, is tagged. It carries the open questions,
origin, `gaps`, `approve` and the generate gate, and the design of tests
derived from a specification. The editor hints and the README's install
line point at it. After the tag, the first step of test generation is
built: derived test cases are ranked by harm and by how often users make
the mistake, the validator warns only for the ones worth writing, and the
test plan lists the rest with the reason.

## The second release tag

`info.version`, since neither the schema's name nor the version the

The tag is annotated, its message names partial specifications with open

## Derived cases ranked by harm and by mistakes

The first of the seven items in `docs/test-generation.md`. A requirement
may name its `harm` (data-loss, money, security, safety, privacy,
availability), and a field or a parameter's schema may say `mistakes:
frequent` or `mistakes: rare`. Every derived case has a frequency from the
table now printed in `docs/conventions.md`, and a rank: critical when its
subject satisfies a requirement with a harm or when it is a failing
dependency, frequent when its frequency is frequent, other otherwise.

`test_case_missing` now warns only for critical and frequent cases. On an
operation with no harm the boundary cases, the 4xx and 5xx responses and
the duplicates drop out of the warnings; a missing required field, a
caller without the permission, a pattern, an enum, a format, a not-found
lookup, a wrong state and a command's exit codes stay. The test plan has a
section "Derived cases left out" with every uncovered case of rank other,
its subject, its scenario and the reason. The traceability document and
chapter 13 of the techspec show a Harm column once a requirement names a
harm, and the requirements document names the harm of each requirement.
A field's `mistakes` shows among its limits in the techspec.

Specification first: SA-21 and SA-22 with NEED-9, ADR-020, ISO/IEC/IEEE
29119-4:2021 as a source, the rule text of `test_case_missing`, `harm` and
`mistakes` in the design schema. Both validator builds rank the same way
and print the same output; the documents are Go only. Six cases lost the
warnings they no longer get, `validate-derived-cases-listed` among them,
and five cases are new: harm making every case chosen, `mistakes` rare and
frequent with a failing channel, an unknown harm, the left-out section,
and the Harm column. In the example, LIB-3 names `harm: [money]`.

Decisions made here, for the owner to confirm or veto:

- A subject takes the harm of its own `satisfies` only. A constraint or a
  transition does not take the harm of its entity, so marking one does
  not mark the rest.
- Every derived case of a critical subject is critical, the golden
  boundary cases too, not only the red ones the design text first named.
- The Harm column appears only when some requirement names a harm, so a
  specification that does not use harm gets no empty column.
- The left-out table has a Scenario column where the design had Rank,
  since every row there has rank other.
- `mistakes` on a parameter's schema covers its `not found` case as well
  as its limits.

Open: the v0.2.0 schema, which the editor hints name, has neither `harm`
nor `mistakes`, so an editor flags both until the next release tag.
