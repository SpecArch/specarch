# Lending rules

## 1 Scope

The library lends books to its members. This document covers loans and fines.

## 2 Loans

2.1 A member shall borrow at most 5 books at a time.

2.2 A loan must be returned within 14 days. A loan that is returned late is charged a fine, and the fine is shown on the member's account at the next visit to the desk.

## 3 Fines

3.1 A fine should be paid at the desk.

It must be paid in cash.
