Open questions, decisions and approval

  specarch gaps <folder>...

prints the open questions document: the counts by priority, the decisions
each stakeholder must take and the material each must provide, then every
question by stage, with who decides, what it blocks, its options and the
decision that opened it; then the references into other specifications
that do not resolve, and which documents and code targets are ready,
drafts or waiting.

  specarch gaps --next <folder>...

prints only the question to ask next in each specification, ranked by
what its answer unblocks, with the decide command that records the
answer; specarch help asking is how to put it to a person.

A question is written in the specification, under questions, when a
source does not say something or two sources disagree:

  questions:
    Q-1:
      question: How many days may a note stay unread before it is archived?
      kind: decision
      priority: must
      decidedBy: writer
      blocks: ["#/entities/Note"]

A must or a should question holds up what it blocks. The stakeholder
answers; the answer is recorded with

  specarch decide Q-1 --by writer --answer "30 days" spec/

which writes the decision record design/decisions/<ID>.yaml under the
next ID, takes the question out, and prints the entries the question
blocked, to be written from the answer; the specification lists design
among its stages and has a design/ folder. Then validate again.

When the documents are written and read, the stakeholder who read them
approves:

  specarch approve --by writer spec/

It refuses a specification with errors, with an open must or should
question, or whose documents differ from what it says now. The record
holds a digest of the files, so any later change voids it. generate runs
only on an approved specification.
