# 2026-11-03

## Item 1692: who said what and when, as records beside the specification

Summary: a system analyst's conversations with the stakeholders, by
meeting, chat, email and call, become records beside the specification
(ADR-220): a communication, a session with its attendance, a test session
and a deploy, each with an instant and its offset and evidence hashed with
SHA-256. A decision cites the communication record of the answer it rests
on. `specarch log` writes one record from options alone and asks for what
is missing; `specarch decide --record` cites it. The validator checks the
links, the evidence and the cited records in both builds and warns of a
role nobody heard. Two documents are written from the records, the
communication history and the timeline. Work began on 2026-11-02 and was
stopped at a savepoint for an OS update (a13afee3); this entry covers the
whole item.

- Record schema: kinds `communication` (`records/communications/`,
  `COM-n`), `session` (`records/sessions/`, `SES-n`), `test-session`
  (`records/test-sessions/`, `TS-n`) and `deploy` (`records/deploys/`,
  `DEP-n`), with `instant` (RFC 3339 with its offset), `words` (form
  verbatim or summary), `evidence` (path under `records/`, media type,
  SHA-256), `links` and `channel`. A communication's own kind is its
  `type`, since `kind` is the record's kind. The records that exist keep
  their dates.
- Design schema: a citation names `record: COM-n` in place of `source`,
  with `says` and no `clause`; an interview source carries `at` and
  `participants`. Both schema evaluators assert `format: date-time`.
- Rules `evidence`, `record_cited` and `unheard`, in Go and Swift, with
  the cases `validate-communications-valid`, `validate-communication-ref`,
  `validate-evidence`, `validate-record-cited` and `validate-unheard`.
  `unheard` is a warning gated on at least one communication, session or
  test session record, so a specification that keeps none warns nothing.
- `specarch log`: every field an option; a missing one is a line on
  standard output, `missing --<option>: ask ...`, with the values the
  specification offers, and `log.missing` with `--json`; refusals name the
  value and what to correct; for an answer the `decide --record` line is
  printed. Eight cases, `log-writes-record` to `log-usage-error`.
- `specarch decide --record <COM-n>`: refused for a record that is not a
  communication record, not an answer or one that does not link the
  question, and with `--source` together; the record is written with
  `cites: [{record: ..., says: ...}]`. Cases `decide-cites-record`,
  `decide-record-refused`, `decide-record-unknown`,
  `decide-source-and-record`.
- Documents `communications` and `timeline` (`internal/generate/
  communications.go`): record documents, never drafts, not in the outputs
  table; a document for everyone leaves the person label and what touches
  an internal element out (`Withheld.CoversRef`, which reads a `#/`
  pointer as the pointers are kept). A date-only event is placed at the
  start of its day in the specification's `timeZone`; one with no date
  yet comes last. Cases `document-writes-communications`,
  `document-writes-timeline`, `document-communications-everyone`.
- Spec: ADR-220 (proposed; rests on ADR-155, ADR-193, ADR-196, ADR-119),
  SA-117 (the records, the rules and the documents) and SA-118 (`log` and
  `decide --record`); the `log`, `decide` and `document` command designs;
  the rule and document-target enums.
- Docs: `docs/maintenance.md` (records table, a section on the four kinds,
  the rules, the documents, the one exception to "the specification never
  points back at its records": a citation of a communication record),
  `docs/conventions.md` (citations with `record`, an interview's `at` and
  `participants`), `docs/help/asking.txt` (recording what the analyst
  reports from a conversation), `docs/help/generate.txt`, the agent facts
  (`AGENTS.md`, the skill), README and the roadmap (section 6, entries 6
  and 7).
- Example `library-lending`: COM-1 to COM-4 (a member's call at 10:15
  asking to renew a loan, the board's note with a hashed evidence file,
  IT's build notice, the desk's objection at the acceptance test), SES-1
  with one role invited and absent, TS-1 (UAT of 0.2.0, passed), DEP-1
  (0.1.0 to production); the `communications` and `timeline` documents
  under `docs/`; every stakeholder is heard, so no `unheard` warning.
  The example has no open question, so it carries no `answer` record; the
  conformance cases do.
- Decisions taken: a citation names the record with a key of its own
  rather than an interview source pointing at it (one declared source per
  chat message would fill `sources`); RFC 3339 with its offset rather than
  RFC 9557 with a zone name (the person typing knows the clock, not the
  zone; a past instant's offset is exact); absent derived from invited and
  present; the person label a key of its own, left out of a document for
  everyone; `log` takes options and asks rather than reading free text.
- Handoff mailbox: empty at the start and at the end; nothing processed.
- Left out on purpose: the acceptance loop's cause `unheard` (ADR-083),
  which is item 1693; a Swift build of the documents (documents are Go
  only, as before). Nothing of any real customer, company or person is in
  the example or the cases.

## Item 1692, review round 2: the leap second, the decided-by check and three small fixes

Summary: the review's five comments settled. The Swift build reads a leap
second as the Go build's schema library does; a decision that answers a
question must be decided by the role that gave the cited answer, checked
by `decide --record` and by both validators under `record_cited`; the
`--element` option's description is whole again in the skill and the
documents; `docs/maintenance.md` counts four document targets; an unused
map left `internal/generate/communications.go`.

- The Swift date-time check accepted `23:59:60` anywhere, the Go build
  only when the time less its offset is 23:59 UTC, where RFC 3339 places a
  leap second (`2026-12-31T23:59:60Z`, `2026-12-31T15:59:60-08:00` and
  `2027-01-01T06:59:60+07:00` pass; `2026-10-31T23:59:60+07:00`, 16:59
  UTC, does not). Swift now takes the offset off the clock as the Go
  library does; the comment says so. Cases: `validate-communication-ref`
  gains COM-3 with a leap second where none falls (refused),
  `validate-communications-valid` gains COM-6 at the leap second that ends
  2026 as a clock at +07:00 reads it (accepted), so the landing check's
  Go-versus-Swift comparison covers both directions.
- `decide Q-1 --by owner --record COM-1` was accepted when COM-1 said
  library-board answered, and the decision then read `decidedBy: owner`
  citing another role's words. `decide` now refuses a record whose `from`
  is not `--by`, naming the `--by` to run (the line `log` printed), and
  `record_cited` reports, on a decision that answers questions, an answer
  record said by another role than `decidedBy`, in both builds with one
  message. Cases: `decide-record-wrong-by`; `validate-record-cited` gains
  ADR-004, decided by owner, citing COM-5 from library-board. The design
  texts (`decide.yaml`, `rule.yaml`, ADR-220, SA-118) and
  `docs/maintenance.md` say so.
- `spec/design/commands/log.yaml`: the `element` option's description was
  a plain scalar, so ` #/` opened a YAML comment and the text ended at "A
  requirement ID or a" in the skill, the manual and the techspec; quoted
  and regenerated.
- `docs/maintenance.md`: "Two targets join `DocumentTarget`" counted four
  bullets; now four.
- `internal/generate/communications.go`: the `byRecord` map was filled
  and discarded; dropped.
- Handoff mailbox: empty; nothing processed.
