# 2026-10-30

## decide's line for a blocked decision names a link validate accepts (item 1574, BUG-065, ADR-208)

A user of 1.3.0 reported that decide and validate disagreed. A must
question blocked a decision (`#/decisions/ADR-001`); decide wrote the new
record and printed that ADR-001 was to carry `origin: decided` and
`decidedIn` the new record. Doing so made validate fail with
`origin_decision`: a decision is not decided by another decision. The
record decide wrote said the same in its consequences.

validate's rule stays. It protects the record model: a decision is built
on or superseded, never made the consequence of another, and the link
from a later decision to an earlier one already has its key, `restsOn`
(ADR-188). decide now writes `restsOn` in the new record for each
decision the question blocked, prints that the new record rests on it,
that the blocked decision keeps its own origin, and that it is superseded
by the new one when the answer replaces it; the consequences say it rests
on the blocked decision. On a meta-model before 1.0, which has no
`restsOn`, decide writes none and says to add it after `specarch upgrade`.
Other blocked entries are printed as before.

New cases: `decide-blocks-decision` (1.0.0), `decide-blocks-decision-earlier`
(0.1), and `validate-decision-rests-on-blocked`, which is what the first
leaves and validates clean. Following the supersede choice instead
(`status: superseded`, `supersededBy`) also validates; that was checked by
hand, not in a case. The report came as a handoff; its project's names
and its scenario were left out, and the cases use a generic library
example.
